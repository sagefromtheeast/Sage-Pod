Privacy policy

Your listening stays on your phone.

Sage Pod has no server of its own. This page lists what the app stores, where it stores it, and every request it sends to anyone else.

  • No accountThere is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
  • No analytics or adsThe app contains no analytics or advertising SDK.
  • Stored on your deviceSubscriptions, history, statistics, downloads and settings.
  • Nothing sent to usThe developer receives none of your data.

Sage Pod is a podcast player for Android and iOS, published as free software under the GNU GPL by its developer. This policy covers the app as published on Google Play and as built from its source code.

What stays on your device

DataWhere it is kept
Subscriptions, episodes, queue, favourites, listening history and statisticsA database on your phone
Downloaded episodesThe app's private storage
SettingsThe same database on your phone
Usernames and passwords for password-protected feedsThe system keystore (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain)
Backups you export (OPML or database)Wherever you choose to save them

Android's own backup ("Back up to Google Drive") may include the app's database if you turned it on in your phone's settings. Feed passwords are left out of that backup. That setting belongs to your phone, not to the app.

Network requests the app makes

Sage Pod contacts other servers only to do something you asked for.

RequestSent toWhat is sent
Refresh a podcast, download or stream an episode, load artwork and chaptersThe podcast's own host, whoever publishes the feedA normal web request for that file: your IP address and the app's user agent (SagePod/…). For a password-protected feed, the username and password you entered, sent only to that feed's own host and never over plain HTTP after HTTPS.
Search for podcasts and browse charts in DiscoverApple's podcast search (itunes.apple.com, rss.applemarketingtools.com) and fyyd (api.fyyd.de)Your search words and your IP address
Check for app updatesExpo's update service (u.expo.dev)The app version, platform, update channel and an anonymous random installation ID used to serve the right update
Open a website, funding link or show-notes linkThat website, in your browserWhatever your browser sends

Podcast hosts, Apple, fyyd and Expo receive these requests directly and handle them under their own privacy policies. Podcast hosts often log downloads to count listeners. The app can't control that.

Notifications

New-episode and download notifications are created on your phone. The app does not use a push-notification service.

Children

Sage Pod is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Inside the app you can also reset statistics, delete downloads and remove podcasts.

Changes

This policy is kept in the app's source repository, so every change to it is public.
